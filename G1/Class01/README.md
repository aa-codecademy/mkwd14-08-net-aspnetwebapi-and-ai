# What is an Internship?

An internship is an opportunity to **learn, improve and grow** in a professional software development environment.

It gives interns the opportunity to transition into a developer position while working alongside experienced engineers who can guide, mentor and support them.

Companies invest in internships because they want to develop future software engineers who understand both technology and teamwork.

That's why they usually look for people who demonstrate:

- Passion for programming
- Curiosity
- Willingness to learn
- Adaptability
- Responsibility
- Teamwork

Remember that an internship is a learning experience. You're **not expected to know everything** from day one.

# What an Internship is NOT

There can't be a successful internship without experienced developers willing to teach and mentor interns.

Interns are **not required to know everything**. That's the difference between an internship and a full developer position.

An internship is **not**:

- Free labour
- A replacement for a full-time developer
- A mistake-free environment

Making mistakes is expected.

Every bug you solve, every code review you receive and every challenge you overcome helps you become a better developer.

The goal isn't perfection—it's continuous learning and improvement.
